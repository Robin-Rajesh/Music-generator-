    Paint music on spinning rings

    Every ring is an instrument. Every dot is a note. Every ring can spin to its own rhythm — and everything stays in key.

    1. 1Tap a ring to drop a note. Tap it again to remove it.
    2. 2Further from the centre means a higher pitch.
    3. 3Change a ring's steps to make polyrhythms, or hit Generate.

    Headphones recommended 🎧
