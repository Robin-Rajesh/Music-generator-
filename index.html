    Orbitals · loop machine no. 01

    Music on a clock face.

    Five rings, five instruments. Tap a ring to add a note. The further out you tap, the higher it plays. It's always in key, so it's hard to make something that sounds bad.

    1. ATap to add, tap again to remove, drag to draw.
    2. BGive rings different step counts and they fall into polyrhythms.
    3. CStuck? Generate writes a loop for you.
